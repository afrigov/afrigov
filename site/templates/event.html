Passport open day, Kano

Renew or apply for a passport in one visit. Walk in, no appointment needed.

When
Saturday 14 November 2026, 8am to 4pm
Where
Kano passport office, 12 Zoo Road, Kano. Map and directions
Who it is for
Anyone renewing a passport, or applying for the first time
Cost
The usual passport fee. The open day itself is free

What happens on the day

Officers from the passport office and the registration office are in one hall. You can confirm your details, have your photograph taken and pay, all in one visit. Most people are done within an hour.

What to bring

  • Your current passport, if you have one
  • Your national ID card
  • A way to pay: card, bank transfer or mobile money

Come before 2pm to be sure of being seen. The hall is on the ground floor, with level access and seating.
