How to renew a passport online

What you need, how to pay, and where to collect your new passport, in two and a half minutes.

Read the transcript

You can renew your passport online if it expired less than five years ago.

You need your current passport, your national ID number, and a way to pay.

Apply online, pay, and choose the office where you will collect it. We will text you when it is ready.

  • Who can renew online
  • What you need before you start
  • Paying, and collecting the new passport
