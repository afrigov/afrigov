Renew a passport

Use this service to renew an adult passport that has expired or will expire in the next 12 months.

It takes about 10 minutes. Standard processing takes 6 weeks.

Before you start

You will need:

  • your current passport
  • your National Identification Number
  • a payment card or mobile money for the fee
Start now
Other ways to apply

You can apply in person at any passport office. Bring the documents above and a printed copy of this page.
