Statement

A message from the head of an organisation: their portrait beside a short text, their name and role, and a link to the full message. For a minister, a director general, a governor, a chair.

When to use

Once on a home page, under the hero, and on the About page with the full text. It is where the portrait belongs. Do not put the portrait in the hero; the hero is about the service, not the person.

When not to use

For quotations inside an article, which are ordinary paragraphs or inset text.

A message from the Director General

Every household should be able to count on clean water and know who to call when it stops. This year we are publishing our inspection results, district by district, so that you can hold us to that.

Dr Amina BelloDirector General, National Water Agency

How it works

  • The title names the role, not the person: "A message from the Minister". The name and role go under the text in ag-statement__by, so screen reader users hear who is speaking after what was said, the way a signed letter reads.
  • The portrait is decorative because the name is written beside it: alt="". Square, 320px is enough, under 40 KB.
  • Keep the text to three sentences. The full message is a page of its own, linked at the end.
  • On phones the portrait stacks above the text.

Accessibility

  • The section is labelled by its title, so it is a landmark people can jump to.
  • The name is in strong on its own line; the role follows in muted text that still meets AA.
