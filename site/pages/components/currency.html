Amounts are written the way the country writes them. The symbol, whether it comes before or after, and how thousands are grouped all come from the pack.

Rules

  • Whole units unless the decimals matter. ₦35,000, not ₦35,000.00.
  • Group thousands the local way. English-style commas in Nigeria, Kenya and Ghana; a thin space in Senegal.
  • The symbol and the number never break across lines. The component keeps them together.
  • In tables, use ag-table__numeric as well so amounts right-align and the digits line up.
  • Say the currency in words once on a page if people may be abroad: "35,000 naira".
