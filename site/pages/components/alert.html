Alert

A message that stays on the page. Every alert has a title, so its meaning never depends on colour.

When to use

To tell people something changed or something matters: a deadline, a payment received, a session about to end.

When not to use

For form validation, use the error summary. For a message that disappears on its own: never. Timed messages fail WCAG and get missed on slow devices.

Applications close on 31 October

Submit before 11:59pm local time.

Payment received

Your receipt number is REF-2026-004512.

Save your progress to avoid losing it.

We could not verify your ID number

Check the number and try again, or contact the registration office.

Accessibility

  • Use role="alert" only for errors people must act on now. It interrupts the screen reader.
  • Use role="status" for everything else. It is announced when the reader is free.
  • The title is a heading at the right level for the page, usually h2 or h3.
  • Links inside an alert use ink, not the primary colour, because the primary is not guaranteed on the tinted background.
  • Put an alert that reports the result of an action at the top of the page the person lands on.
