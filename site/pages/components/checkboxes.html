Accessibility

  • Group related checkboxes in a fieldset with the question as the legend. Say "Select all that apply" in the hint.
  • A single confirmation needs no fieldset; the label is the question.
  • Never pre-tick a consent box. It is both bad practice and, in many countries, unlawful.
  • The tick is drawn in white, which passes on every pack's primary because the build guarantees it.
