People

The people who lead an organisation: a portrait, a name and a role for each. A governing board, a management team, the ministers of a ministry.

When to use

On an About or leadership page, for three people or thirty. Group them under headings: the board, then management.

When not to use

For one person with a message, use the statement. For a staff directory with phone numbers and departments, use a table.

  • Dr Amina Bello

    Director General

  • Mr Kofi Mensah

    Deputy Director General, Operations

  • Ms Wanjiru Kamau

    Director, Finance

  • Mr Ousmane Diallo

    Director, Inspections

Variant: rows

For a long list, each person takes one row, with a small round portrait at the start.

  • Dr Amina Bello

    Chair

  • Mr Kofi Mensah

    Member

  • Ms Wanjiru Kamau

    Member

How it works

  • A list, so a screen reader says how many people there are.
  • The portrait is square and cropped to fit. Use photographs taken the same way, with the face in the middle.
  • The name is a heading at the level the page needs. Make it a link only if the person has a page of their own.
  • Titles such as Dr, Hon. and Alhaji are part of the name. Write the role in full: "Deputy Director General, Operations", never an abbreviation.
  • If you have no photograph for some people, leave the portrait out for all of them and use the rows variant.

Accessibility

  • The portrait's alt is empty, because the name is beside it. A screen reader would otherwise say every name twice.
  • The role is real text under the name, never part of the image.
