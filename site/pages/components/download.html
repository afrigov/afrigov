Rules

  • Format in capitals, then size, in brackets at the end: (PDF, 2.4 MB). Round sizes to one decimal.
  • Say the size, because many people pay per megabyte. If you do not know it, find out; "unknown" is not acceptable on a government site.
  • Prefer an HTML page to a PDF. A PDF is for something people need to print or keep: a form, a signed document, a long report.
  • Add download to the link only for files that should save rather than open, like forms to fill in.
  • The icon is drawn in CSS and carries no meaning; the words do.
