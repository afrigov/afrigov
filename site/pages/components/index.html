Components

Thirty-nine building blocks. Each is a class on a plain element, works without JavaScript, and is tested with axe with every country pack on every pull request.

Page furniture

  • Skip link: the first thing a keyboard reaches.
  • Official website banner: flag and official-site notice above the header.
  • Header and navigation: organisation name and primary navigation.
  • Footer: link columns and legal bar.
  • Hero: the top of a home page, with an optional image beside the text.
  • Band: a full-width section with a tinted, primary or dark background.
  • Breadcrumb: where this page sits.
  • Back link: the previous step of a form.
  • Pagination: moving through a long list.
  • Language switcher: this page in each official language.
  • Social links: the organisation's accounts, in the footer.

Actions

  • Button: primary, secondary, warning and start.

Forms

  • Text input: one line of text, with label, hint and error.
  • Textarea: more than one line.
  • Character count: a textarea with a limit people can see.
  • Select: one choice from a long list.
  • Radios: one choice from a short list.
  • Checkboxes: any number of choices.
  • Input group: a prefix or suffix, for money and units.
  • Phone number: dialling code from the pack, national number.
  • National ID number: label, hint, length and pattern from the pack.
  • Region selector: region then sub-region, divisions from the pack.
  • Date input: day, month, year.
  • Error summary: every error on the page, at the top.

Content and feedback

  • Alert: information, success, warning, error.
  • Badge: a status word.
  • Dated list: news, events and documents with a date under each link.
  • Download link: a file link that says the format and size.
  • Empty state: a listing with nothing to list yet.
  • Image and figure: captioned photographs and a gallery grid.
  • Statement: a message from the head of the organisation, with their portrait.
  • Details: one disclosure for help some people need.
  • Accordion: sections that open and close.
  • Currency display: amounts written the local way.
  • Table: rows and columns of data.
  • Confirmation panel: the end of a service.
  • Summary list: check your answers.
  • Service card: a list of services, whole card clickable.
  • Inset text: a callout inside content.

Not in the system, on purpose

No modal, no toast, no carousel, no date picker. Each is cut with a reason in decision 003.
