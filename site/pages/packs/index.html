Country packs

A pack is one JSON file. It declares the official national colours and which of them is the primary. The build derives everything else, checks every colour pair, and writes a stylesheet.

What a pack carries

  • Official colours, exact, with their source. Used for the flag and decoration.
  • A primary, derived to pass WCAG AA as text if the official value does not.
  • Flag stripes and their direction.
  • Banner strings in each official language, with a default language.
  • Country data the examples and the 0.2 inputs read: currency, national ID, regions, time zone.

Add your country

Copy tokens/packs/ke.tokens.json, change the values, run pnpm test, open a pull request. The contrast tests run for you. Step by step instructions are in Contributing. Packs wanted: Rwanda, South Africa, Morocco, Uganda, Tanzania, Ethiopia, Côte d'Ivoire, Mozambique.
