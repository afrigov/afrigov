How it works

  • The filters are ag-filters: links to the same page with a topic, such as videos.html?topic=projects, or to a page per topic. The current one has aria-current="page". They work with no script and can be bookmarked and shared.
  • Each card is a video card linking to the video's own page. Newest first.
  • Twelve videos a page, then pagination.
  • No players on this page. A grid of real players would load several megabytes before anyone pressed anything.
  • The video's own page is the video page template: the player, what it covers, the transcript, related videos.
