# v0.9: Country colour and service pages

**Goal:** a site in Ghana should not look like a site in Nigeria, and a service agency should have what its pages are made of. The second use case, Ghana's National Identification Authority, showed both gaps: five of the seven packs have a green primary and nothing used the accent, and every service page needed steps.

## Scope

- Flag stripe under the header, and on its own.
- The accent colour put to work: an accent band with checked text colour, an accent edge on cards and key figures.
- Feature: one thing described, with a picture.
- People: a board or a management team.
- Steps and key figures.
- A flush main element for pages built from bands.
- A secondary button that stays secondary on a coloured band.
- All sixteen regions in the Ghana pack.

## Left open

- A contents list for long pages (issue 29).
- A pattern for finding one entry in a long list (issue 27).

## Exit criteria

- Each new component on a docs page with an example, axe passing for every pack.
- The Ghana rebuild uses the stripe, the feature, people, steps and key figures, with no page-local CSS.
