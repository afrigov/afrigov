**Goal:** the three things the fmcide.gov.ng home page and footer needed that 0.5 did not cover.

## Scope

- Statement: a message from the head of the organisation with their portrait. Named for the thing, not the title, because an agency has a director general and a state has a governor.
- Social links, footer only, name as text and icon decorative.
- Footer address slot, with the italic `address` reset in core.

- All three on the docs, axe passing for every pack, in the registry, used by the rebuild's home page and footer.
