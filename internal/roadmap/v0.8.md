# v0.8: Variants

**Goal:** a second site built on afrigov should not have to look like the first. The fmcide rebuild used one look: white sections, light header, dark footer, lists. 0.8 adds the alternatives at the points that decide a page's look.

## Scope

- Band: tint, primary, dark. The biggest lever; one band changes a page's rhythm.
- Hero: centred, image-first; and inside a band for colour.
- Service card: tinted, plain, image, horizontal.
- Footer light, statement primary, pagination simple, gallery 2 and 4, panel neutral.

## Not variants

Back link, breadcrumb, skip link, banner, alerts, badges, forms. One look each, on purpose. Variation there is a bug waiting to happen.

## Exit criteria

- Every variant on its component's docs page with an example, axe passing for every pack.
- The second use case, in a different country, uses at least the band, a card variant and a different header and footer combination.
